# 修訂版 cover letter 範本（Markdown／Word 版）

用途：修訂稿重新送出時附給編輯的信。只摘要修訂範圍並指向逐點回覆，不重複逐點內容，不在信中辯解未解決的意見。長度以一頁為限。缺少的事實保留可見佔位符。

---

## 英文版（English version）

```markdown
[Date]

Dear Dr. [Editor surname],

Re: Manuscript [ID to be supplied], "[Manuscript title to be supplied]"

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript for [Journal name to be supplied]. We are grateful
to you and to the reviewers for the constructive comments, which have helped us improve the clarity and
rigour of the paper.

In this revision we have:

- reported the System Usability Scale results with sample size, mean, and 95% confidence interval, and
  reframed the usability claim as formative evidence for the first design-based research iteration;
- expanded the Methods to describe the retrieval-augmented generation safeguards, the curated source corpus,
  and the human review step, and added a statement on the system's intended educational use;
- clarified the qualitative analysis procedure, including the data saturation criterion for the
  think-aloud and interview data;
- [further major revision action to be supplied or delete this line].

A point-by-point response to each reviewer's comments is provided in the accompanying response
documents. Changes are marked in [red text / tracked changes] in the marked copy of the manuscript; a clean
copy is also provided.

The revised manuscript has been approved by all authors. It is not under consideration elsewhere, and the
ethics approval ([IRB name and number to be supplied]) and reporting statements are unchanged unless
noted in the response.

We hope the revised manuscript is now suitable for further consideration by [Journal name]. Thank you for
your time.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding author name, credentials]
[Affiliation]
[Email]
on behalf of all authors
```

## 繁中版（給中文期刊）

```markdown
〔日期〕

〔期刊名稱〕編輯委員會 鈞鑒：

主旨：稿件〔編號待補〕「〔題目待補〕」修訂稿送審

感謝貴刊給予本稿修改機會，並感謝審查委員提供的寶貴意見。本次修訂主要包括：

一、結果一節補充系統可用性量表（SUS）之樣本數、平均數與 95% 信賴區間，並將可用性結論調整為設計本位研究第一輪迭代之形成性證據。
二、方法一節補充檢索增強生成（RAG）之知識來源、防範幻覺之設計與人工審核流程，並說明本系統之教學用途定位。
三、方法一節補充質性資料（放聲思考與半結構式訪談）之分析程序與飽和判準。
四、〔其他主要修訂，或刪除本行〕。

各審查委員意見之逐條回覆詳見隨附之修訂說明表。修改處已於修改稿以〔紅字／底線〕標示，並另附清稿一份。中英文摘要已同步修訂。

本稿經全體作者同意送出，未同時投稿其他期刊；研究倫理審查〔IRB 名稱與案號待補〕與相關聲明無變更。

敬請惠予審查。

〔通訊作者姓名〕 敬上
〔服務單位〕
〔聯絡電子郵件〕
```

---

## 填寫規則

1. 條列的修訂動作以三到五項為限，只寫主要修訂；細節留給逐點回覆。
2. 不在 cover letter 裡對審稿人的個別意見表達不同意；若有重大未解決事項，只寫「相關限制已於回覆中說明」。
3. 稿件編號、編輯姓名、IRB 案號未提供時保留佔位符，不可猜。
4. 需要 Word 檔時交 `docx` skill；信頭與簽名依機構格式。
5. 中文版標點用全形；英文版依英文規則，避免破折號與冒號作為句間連接。
